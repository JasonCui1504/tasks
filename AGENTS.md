This version has breaking changes — APIs, conventions, and file structure may all differ from your training data. Read the relevant guide in `node_modules/next/dist/docs/` before writing any code. Heed deprecation notices.

# Tasks

Personal task tracker served at **https://tasks.jascui.com**, installable on the iPhone Home Screen as a web app.

- One page (`/`, `app/Dashboard.tsx`) plus `/login`. API under `/api/*`.
- The page is two layouts of the same data. Computer (≥900px): `app/Grid.tsx`, a window-filling 24×16 grid of movable/resizable boxes, each showing a chosen view; layout saved to the account (`settings.grid_layout`, `/api/layout`) with localStorage (`tasks.grid.v3`) as a fallback; boxes push/shrink each other and resize from any edge or corner; each box's title is a menu of views grouped by heading (Tasks, Task categories, Calendar, Travel, People & money). The top bar (`app/TopInfo.tsx`) has the time-of-day message (`lib/greeting.ts`), clocks, Open-Meteo weather (fetched by the browser) for where you are now (browser location named via BigDataCloud's keyless reverse geocoder, falling back to DC; hidden when you're in Boulder or Stanford), Boulder and Stanford and the next-trip countdown. The Day view (`app/DayView.tsx`) shows `selectedDay` (shared by every calendar) midnight to midnight, including trip itineraries; drag on it (30-minute steps) to type a task or event in place, drag a task/event to move it. Events have an optional `ends_at`. Phone: fixed tabs (Today `app/Today.tsx`, All tasks `app/TasksTab.tsx`, Calendar `app/CalendarView.tsx`, Events, Trips `app/Trips.tsx`, Stay in touch, Gift cards) under the red Urgent callout (`app/Urgent.tsx`). Compact box views are in `app/Panels.tsx`. Every view gets `Data`/`Actions` from `app/shared.tsx`, so an edit opens the same sheet wherever it starts.
- Payments and subscriptions aren't ticked off: the row's square button (or the sheet) **acknowledges** them — a repeating one moves to its next date — and **Later**/Defer quiets one for a day/3 days/a week, then reminds. Either way `tasks.quiet_until` keeps it greyed out, out of Urgent and the morning summary, and silent until then (an acknowledged one comes back at the next date's first reminder, or for a payment on the 1st of the month it's next due if that's sooner). `lib/tasks.ts` `acknowledgeTask`/`deferTask`/`unquietTask`.
- Subscriptions also renew on their own (`isSubscription` in `lib/types.ts`): one not acknowledged rolls to its next renewal in the cron once its date passes (a one-off one clears itself), and they stay out of to-do lists, Overdue/Today counts, Urgent and the badge (Today shows them under "Renewing today"; All tasks only under the Subscriptions filter).
- Lists: Tasks, Events (`app/Events.tsx`, `/api/events`), Stay in touch (`app/Contacts.tsx`, `/api/contacts`; each catch-up is a row in `contact_log`, and `last_contact_on` always follows the latest one) and Gift cards (`app/GiftCards.tsx`, `/api/gift-cards`). Packing list (`app/Packing.tsx`, box view only, `/api/packing`): trips named here (not linked to travel trips), each with its own items to tick off; the box lists just the trips until one is opened. These are stored by `lib/lists.ts`; their reminders and expiry alerts live in `lib/notify.ts` next to the task ones.
- Data: its own Turso/libSQL database (`TASKS_DATABASE_URL`/`TASKS_DATABASE_AUTH_TOKEN`); locally `.data/tasks.db`. Schema is created on first use (`lib/db.ts`).
- Login: scrypt hashes in `task_users`, falling back to `TASKS_USERS`. While neither has a user, `/login` shows a one-time "Create your account" form guarded by a setup code (the `CRON_SECRET` value); `node scripts/set-password.mjs <user> [--env file]` also sets or resets a password. Sessions are signed with `TASKS_SESSION_SECRET`.
- Notifications: Web Push (VAPID keys, `npm run vapid`). `lib/notify.ts` decides what to send; `/api/cron` runs it, called every ~10 min by `.github/workflows/notify.yml` with `CRON_SECRET`. Each notification is keyed in `notification_log` so it fires once. Twice-daily summaries (`lib/brief.ts`, shared by server and browser): morning (default 9 AM: overdue, today, payments & renewals in the next 7 days with a total, the week ahead) and evening (default 9 PM: what's still open, tomorrow, payments & renewals, later this week); times are `settings.morning_hour`/`evening_hour` (Settings sheet; null = off), sent even in quiet hours. Tapping one opens `/?brief=morning|evening`, the `BriefSheet` (`app/Brief.tsx`); it's also the "Daily summary" box view and a link atop the phone Today tab.
- Trips: read-only from travel.jascui.com. Names/dates come from `/api/feed` with `TRAVEL_FEED_SECRET` (the same secret Llamabear uses). The dashboard refetches everything on load, when the app returns to the foreground and every 3 minutes while it's on screen; the server caches the travel feed and itineraries for 1 minute. Itineraries for the Day view come from each trip's existing calendar feed (`/api/trips/<id>/calendar.ics`), read with the owner's own travel sign-in: they connect once in the Trips box with their travel username/password; `lib/itinerary.ts` keeps the travel session (and the password AES-GCM-encrypted with a key derived from `TASKS_SESSION_SECRET`, only to renew the 30-day session) in `travel_link`, and Disconnect deletes it. `lib/ics.ts` parses the feed. Upcoming (`app/Tracking.tsx`, view id `tracking` so saved layouts keep working; a box view, and its own phone tab) is one list of flights, trains, rental cars, hotels and free-cancellation deadlines, grouped by trip: bookings entered here (`bookings` table, `/api/bookings`, `app/BookingSheet.tsx`; local wall-clock dates/times as on the ticket; `cancel_by` alerts in `lib/notify.ts`) merged with the travel itineraries (read-only; a flight on both shows once, as the one entered here). **Still to book** shows bookings entered as "Still to book" plus items marked "Not booked" on the travel app (its unconfirmed stays/cars and Budget to-dos aren't in the calendar feed). Nothing is ever written to the travel app (repo JasonCui1504/travel_budget); changes there need the owner's permission.
